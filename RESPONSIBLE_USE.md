# Responsible use of ASG

ASG is intended to support lawful software engineering and learning. Users are
responsible for assessing their instructions, generated outputs, dependencies,
data access and deployment against applicable laws, third-party rights and the
terms of the services they use. Model output and passing tests do not establish
that a project is lawful, safe, accurate or suitable for a particular purpose.

Do not use ASG to facilitate unlawful access, fraud, privacy violations or other
illegal activity. This is responsible-use guidance, not an additional condition
on the permissions in [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE). It does not amend that
license, bind independent forks to a separate acceptable-use contract, or claim
that technical safeguards can prevent every misuse.

The warranty disclaimer and liability limitation are in sections 6 and 7 of the
license and apply subject to applicable law and any separate agreement. They
are not a guarantee of immunity from legal claims. Nothing in this document
makes a claim about responsibility in a particular case or jurisdiction.

ASG's current workflow requires explicit approvals and bounded supported
operations. These controls protect workflow and execution boundaries; they are
not proof that a user's purpose or generated program is lawful. Review outputs,
permissions and dependencies before running or distributing them. Do not treat
model explanations as legal advice.

Any future hosted service operated by the project should have separately
reviewed service-specific terms, acceptable-use rules and abuse-handling
procedures before launch. Those service terms would govern that service, not
silently restrict the open-source software grant. User-owned Hugging Face
resources remain subject to the user's provider terms and account controls.

Report vulnerabilities through [the security policy](SECURITY.md). Do not post
credentials, private data or harmful working payloads in public reports.
